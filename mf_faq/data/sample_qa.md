**Q. What is the expense ratio of Groww Value Fund?**

> The Total Expense Ratio (TER) is the annual recurring cost charged to a scheme, expressed as a percentage of its assets and reflected in the NAV. Groww Mutual Fund publishes the current TER for every scheme and plan on its expense-ratio page, updated daily; because TER is revised from time to time, this assistant links you to the live figure rather than quoting one that may be stale.
>
> Source: [Expense Ratio (TER) disclosure - Groww Mutual Fund](https://www.growwmf.in/downloads/expense-ratio) — Groww Mutual Fund (AMC)
>

**Q. What is the exit load of Groww Value Fund?**

> Groww Value Fund charges an exit load of 1% if units are redeemed or switched out within 1 year from the date of allotment. There is no exit load if redeemed or switched out after 1 year.
>
> Source: [Scheme Information Document - Groww Value Fund](https://assets-netstorage.growwmf.in/compliance_docs/Downloads/SID/SID_Groww%20Value%20Fund.pdf) — Groww Mutual Fund (AMC)
